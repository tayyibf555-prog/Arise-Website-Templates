FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Bring your questions to the call, or email hello@haldenstudio.co.

(01)Is the website designed by AI?

AI speeds up research, first drafts and the repetitive parts of the build. Every design and every line of copy is reviewed and finished by a senior person on our team.

(02)How much does it cost?

Most builds land between £4,950 and £12,000 depending on pages and integrations. You get a fixed quote on the call, and it doesn’t change.

(03)Which platform do you build on?

Webflow, Framer or Next.js, whichever suits your team. We’ll recommend one on the call based on who needs to edit the site.

(04)Do I own the website?

Yes. The design, code, copy and accounts are all yours from day one. No lock-in and no licence fees.

(05)What do you need from me?

About two hours across the two weeks: the kickoff call, two short reviews and final sign-off. We handle everything else.

(06)What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of care included. After that, most clients keep us on a monthly growth plan for new pages and testing, but there’s no obligation.
