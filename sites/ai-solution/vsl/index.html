Questions

Before you book

What kind of work can agents take on?

Anything repetitive that follows a pattern: qualifying leads, answering routine emails, reconciling data, writing reports, chasing documents and updating systems.

Is our data safe?

Agents run with the minimum permissions they need, every action is logged, and nothing is used to train public models. We sign an NDA and DPA before we start.

Do we need a technical team?

No. We build, host and maintain everything. Your team approves drafts and tells us what to automate next.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Every workflow, prompt and integration is documented and handed over, and you can take it in-house whenever you like.

How is pricing structured?

A fixed build fee per workflow, then a monthly fee for hosting, monitoring and improvements. We'll quote on the audit call.
